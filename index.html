About The System

How PathFinder Pro Empowers Students

Designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations through structured self-assessment and actionable career roadmaps.

1. Interest & Skill Mapping

Evaluates your favorite subjects, problem-solving style, and cognitive preferences through a multi-dimensional questionnaire.

2. Weighted Scoring Engine

Uses JavaScript decision logic to calculate affinity scores across Software, Data Science, Cybersecurity, UI/UX, Business, and Marketing.

3. Deep Career Insights

Provides transparent breakdowns of job roles, required technical qualifications, core tools, and industry salary expectations.

4. Actionable Roadmaps

Guides students from beginner fundamentals to capstone projects and industry certifications with curated course tracks.
